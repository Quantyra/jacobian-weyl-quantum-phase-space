# INTEGRITY — Standing claims and non-claims boundary

This repository is the science and packaging surface for the Quantyra
Jacobian / Weyl / quantum phase-space program (planning epic E005).

## Standing boundary

Until expanded under an approved claim-boundary protocol with independent
human approval:

1. **Pinned finite identities, without discovery or priority.** This project
   affirms the exact determinant and collision identities for the pinned
   Keller map stated in Theorem A and checked in the companion artifacts. It
   stands behind the bounded mathematical consequences stated for that map.
   It does not claim discovery or historical priority, adjudicate unrelated
   proposed examples, or transfer these checks to a differently normalized or
   modified map.
2. **No new quantum algorithms or hardware claims.** This project does not
   claim new quantum algorithms, complexity separations, or hardware results.
3. **No physical gate claims without criteria and proof.** This project does
   not claim that exotic Weyl endomorphisms are physical quantum gates
   (unitaries, CCR automorphisms, or symplectic-preserving maps) without an
   explicit reversibility / unitarity / automorphism / symplectic criterion
   and supporting proof or obstruction.
4. **No P-vs-NP.** This project does not claim P-vs-NP results, NP/circuit
   lower bounds, Frege/PHP results, or AC0 collapse results.

## Allowed public language

- Bounded theorem language tied to pinned sources and exact companion
  declarations.
- Literature maps, definitions, negative/obstruction results, citation
  packages, and methodology notes.
- Statements that distinguish external attribution of the object of study
  from the internally verified pinned identities.

## Forbidden public language

- “We discovered the counterexample” or an unsupported priority claim.
- “This yields a new quantum gate / algorithm / speedup.”
- “Exotic endomorphisms are unitary” without criteria and evidence.
- Any P-vs-NP or circuit-complexity claim derived from this lane.

## Specific A001 boundaries

- The artifact constructs the displayed polynomial Weyl endomorphism
  \(\psi\), but does not establish that \(\psi\) is noninvertible and does not
  claim a new rank-three Dixmier counterexample.
- The Lean artifact proves an injection from unitary complex phases into the
  self-adjoint-extension witnesses. The continuum-sized lower-family
  conclusion additionally uses the classical cardinality of the complex unit
  circle; that cardinal identification is not one of the cited Lean
  declarations.
- No preferred extension, physical selection rule, exact cardinality of the
  full extension type, or exact Hamel-rank equality is claimed.
- The current operator-theory boundary is H1-only. Exact H0/H2 deficiency
  indices, H0/H2 essential-self-adjointness conclusions, and dual-flow
  strong-CCR obstruction/no-go statements in older validation documents are
  withdrawn as current theorems and remain open pending Lean.
