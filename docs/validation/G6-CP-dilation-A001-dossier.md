# G6 CP / dilations / channels — A001

**Status:** superseded 2026-07-29; no CP/channel construction, and the former
explicit-\(\psi\) nonautomorphy certification is withdrawn
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Story:** S022

## Setup
G3 gives a **unital algebra endomorphism** \(\psi\) of the polynomial Weyl algebra (multiplicative, preserves \(*\) formally when \(B\) real and div-free).

## Facts
1. Multiplicative \(\psi\) is **not** automatically a Heisenberg-picture quantum channel on \(B(H)\).  
2. Channels require **normal unital CP** maps on a von Neumann algebra (or CP on \(C^*\)).  
3. **Stinespring** dilations apply to CP maps; algebra endomorphisms that extend to automorphisms are implemented by unitaries (if inner); **proper** endomorphisms need correspondence/Cuntz/Hilbert-module pictures (charter WP4).  
4. **Withdrawn historical inference:** classical non-injectivity of \(\Phi\)
   and noninjectivity of \(F\) do not, by themselves, prove that the displayed
   rank-three Weyl endomorphism \(\psi\) is non-surjective or not an
   automorphism. This dossier does not decide explicit-\(\psi\)
   automorphy/nonautomorphy.

## Verdict
- **No CP/channel claim authorized.**  
- If a future G4/G5 extension exists and is CP, its reversibility or subsystem
  interpretation must be proved independently; it cannot be inferred from the
  seed collision.
- G6 remains **open / not constructed**. No reversible-gate, channel, or
  nonautomorphy conclusion follows from this dossier.
